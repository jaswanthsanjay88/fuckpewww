Setup Guide

Install on Laptop

  1. Click Download ZIP and save the file.
  2. Extract it to a folder you will not move or delete.
  3. Open Chrome, Edge, or Brave and go to chrome://extensions.
  4. Enable Developer mode from the top-right corner.
  5. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder.
  6. Open your Parul E-Learning PDF page and refresh once.
  7. Click the floating black button when it appears to download the PDF.

If the Button Is Not Showing

  • Check that the extension is enabled in chrome://extensions.
  • Refresh the PDF page with Ctrl+Shift+R.
  • Confirm you are on the supported learning portal domains.
  • Keep Developer mode on for unpacked extensions.
